Quick Glossary

Premium

The amount you pay monthly or annually for your health insurance coverage, regardless of whether you use medical services.

Deductible

The amount you must pay out-of-pocket before your insurance starts covering medical expenses.

Copay

A fixed amount you pay for specific medical services, like doctor visits or prescriptions, at the time of service.

Network

Healthcare providers (hospitals, doctors, clinics) that have contracts with your insurance company to provide services at negotiated rates.

Sum Insured

The maximum amount your insurance company will pay for covered medical expenses during a policy year.

NCB (No Claim Bonus)

A discount or benefit you receive for not making any claims during a policy year, often increasing your sum insured.
